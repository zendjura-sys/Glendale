## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

GameCon is a social network built for gamers: friends, families and clans, game servers, chats, music and movies in one app. One account for gamecon.online and your phone — everything you do here shows up on PC instantly.

🎮 FEED & FRIENDS
• Posts with photos, GIFs, stickers, polls, and cards for games, tracks and movies
• 24-hour moments, comments and reactions
• "Ready to play" — friends see you're free and invite you to a match
• Find people, communities, games and servers in one search

🛡 FAMILY HQ FOR GTA RP
• Treasury and bank with full transaction history
• Weapons and supplies warehouse that warns you when stock runs low
• Contract reports with anti-fake checks: a reused screenshot is flagged instantly
• Discipline, ranks, events, leaderboard and an action log
• Family chat with short AI summaries, plus voice rooms
• Family notifications in Discord and Telegram

🖥 SERVERS & CLANS
• Server catalog with live online count, voting and news
• Clans, tournaments, party and teammate finder

💬 CHATS
• Private and group chats, community channels, reactions, threads, voice messages and calls

🎵 MUSIC
• Your tracks and playlists, radio stations, charts and lyrics
• "Listen together" — play the same track in sync with friends

🎬 MOVIES
• Catalog of movies, series and anime, your own lists, new-episode schedule, ratings and discussions

⭐ QUESTS & SEASON
• Daily quests, day streaks, a season pass and profile rewards

🇺🇦 FOR UKRAINE
• Air-raid alert map by region and district, with an alert widget in the menu

🎨 LOOK & FEEL
• Frosted-glass themes "Neon Blue" and "Arctic Ice", light and dark, Ukrainian, English and other languages

GameCon+ is an optional subscription with extra profile features; in the app it is purchased through Google Play.
